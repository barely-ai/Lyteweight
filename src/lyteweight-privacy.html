Lyteweight Privacy Policy

Lyteweight is a personal training app used by its owner. It is a modified version of Liftosaur.

Workout data

Workouts, programs and measurements are stored on your device. Lyteweight has no accounts and no analytics. Only the optional Community features below send anything, and only what they describe.

WHOOP and Withings

If you connect WHOOP or Withings, Lyteweight asks for read-only access. The login tokens are stored in the owner's Cloudflare account and are used only to copy your recovery, sleep, strain, workout and body measurement data to your Lyteweight app. The data is not sold, shared or used for anything else.

You can disconnect at any time in Lyteweight under Me → Connected Devices, which deletes the stored tokens. You can also revoke access from your WHOOP or Withings account.

Community

The Community library is optional. Programs you publish, your comments and the display name you choose are public to everyone using Lyteweight. Your phone keeps a private key that lets only it edit or remove what you published.

If you allow anonymous usage sharing, finishing a workout of a community program sends that program's id, a random install id and the week number. It ranks programs by how long people run them. You can turn it off under Me → Community.

If you join the leaderboard, finishing a workout sends your best squat, bench press and deadlift, the kilograms you lifted, your cardio minutes and calories, your bodyweight and your sex. Others see your display name, weight class and scores, never your exact bodyweight. You can leave the leaderboard at any time.

You can report programs, comments and leaderboard entries, and hide a person's comments on your phone. Reported content is reviewed by the owner.
